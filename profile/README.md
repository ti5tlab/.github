## About Us

**TI5Robot Technology Innovation Lab** is dedicated to the research, development, and innovation of robotics technologies.

Building on the robotics technology ecosystem of TI5Robot, we explore the integration of robotic hardware, intelligent control systems, motion algorithms, and software platforms.

We are committed to sharing engineering experience, development tools, technical resources, and open-source projects with developers and researchers worldwide.

Our mission is to make robotics development more accessible, efficient, and collaborative.

## Research & Development

We focus on the following areas of robotics technology:

| Research Area | Description |
|---|---|
| 🤖 Humanoid Robotics | Humanoid robot systems, motion control and intelligent applications |
| ⚙️ Robotic Actuators | Joint modules, motor drivers and actuator control systems |
| 🦾 Robotic Manipulation | Robotic arms, dexterous hands and force control |
| 🎮 Motion Control | Motor control, trajectory planning and real-time control |
| 🧠 Embodied Intelligence | Robot perception, intelligent decision-making and embodied AI |
| 🔗 Robot Teleoperation | Motion capture, retargeting and human-robot interaction |
| 🛠️ Robotics Software | SDKs, middleware, development tools and system integration |

## Open Source Projects

We believe open source is an important driving force behind robotics innovation.

Our projects cover:

- **Hardware & Drivers** — Hardware interfaces, motor drivers and communication protocols.
- **Motion Control** — Motion algorithms, trajectory generation and control systems.
- **ROS & ROS 2** — Robotics middleware, packages and integration examples.
- **Robot SDKs** — Development interfaces, APIs and software tools.
- **Teleoperation** — Remote control, motion retargeting and human-robot interaction.
- **AI & Robotics** — Computer vision, intelligent algorithms and embodied AI.

Explore our repositories to discover tools, examples and ongoing projects.
