## 🌐 About Us | 关于我们

**TI5Robot Technology Innovation Lab** is dedicated to the research, development, and innovation of robotics technologies.

Building on the robotics technology ecosystem of TI5Robot, we explore the integration of robotic hardware, intelligent control systems, motion algorithms, and software platforms.

We are committed to sharing engineering experience, development tools, technical resources, and open-source projects with developers and researchers worldwide.

**TI5Robot 科技创新实验室** 致力于机器人相关技术的研究、开发与创新。

依托钛虎机器人的机器人技术生态，我们持续探索机器人硬件、智能控制系统、运动算法与软件平台的融合应用。

我们希望通过开源项目、技术文档、开发工具和工程实践，与全球开发者及研究人员共同推动机器人技术的发展。

## 🚀 Research & Development | 研发方向

We focus on the following areas of robotics technology:

我们重点关注以下机器人技术方向：

| Research Area 研发方向 | Description 技术内容 |
|---|---|
| 🤖 Humanoid Robotics 人形机器人 | Humanoid robot systems, motion control and intelligent applications. 人形机器人系统、运动控制与智能应用。 |
| ⚙️ Robotic Actuators 机器人执行器 | Joint modules, motor drivers and actuator control. 关节模组、电机驱动与执行器控制。 |
| 🦾 Robotic Manipulation 机器人操作 | Robotic arms, dexterous hands and force control. 机械臂、灵巧手与力控技术。 |
| 🎮 Motion Control 运动控制 | Motor control, trajectory planning and real-time control. 电机控制、轨迹规划与实时控制。 |
| 🧠 Embodied Intelligence 具身智能 | Robot perception, intelligent decision-making and embodied AI. 机器人感知、智能决策与具身智能。 |
| 🔗 Robot Teleoperation 机器人遥操作 | Motion capture, retargeting and human-robot interaction. 动作捕捉、动作映射与人机交互。 |
| 🛠️ Robotics Software 机器人软件 | SDKs, middleware, development tools and system integration. SDK、中间件、开发工具与系统集成。 |

## 💻 Open Source Projects | 开源项目

We believe that open source is an important driving force behind robotics innovation.

Our open-source projects cover multiple aspects of robotics development:

我们相信开源是推动机器人技术创新的重要力量。

我们的开源项目涵盖机器人开发的多个领域：

- **Hardware & Drivers | 硬件与驱动**  
  Hardware interfaces, motor drivers and communication protocols.  
  硬件接口、电机驱动及通信协议。

- **Motion Control | 运动控制**  
  Motion algorithms, trajectory generation and control systems.  
  运动算法、轨迹生成与控制系统。

- **ROS & ROS 2 | 机器人中间件**  
  ROS packages, robot applications and integration examples.  
  ROS 软件包、机器人应用及系统集成示例。

- **Robot SDKs | 机器人开发工具**  
  SDKs, APIs and development utilities.  
  SDK、API 接口及开发工具。

- **Teleoperation | 机器人遥操作**  
  Remote control, motion retargeting and human-robot interaction.  
  远程控制、动作映射与人机交互。

- **AI & Robotics | 人工智能与机器人**  
  Computer vision, intelligent algorithms and embodied AI.  
  计算机视觉、智能算法与具身智能。

## 🤝 Community & Collaboration | 开源协作

We welcome developers, engineers, researchers, and robotics enthusiasts to join our open-source community.

Whether you are interested in robot hardware, motion control, intelligent algorithms, or robotics applications, your ideas and contributions are welcome.

我们欢迎开发者、工程师、研究人员及机器人爱好者加入开源社区。

无论你关注机器人硬件、运动控制、智能算法还是机器人应用开发，我们都期待与你交流和合作。

**How to Contribute | 如何参与**

1. ⭐ Star our repositories and explore our projects.  
   Star 项目，了解我们的开源成果。

2. 🐛 Submit Issues and share technical suggestions.  
   提交 Issue，反馈问题与技术建议。

3. 🔧 Contribute code through Pull Requests.  
   通过 Pull Request 参与代码贡献。

4. 📚 Improve documentation and share development experiences.  
   完善技术文档，分享开发经验。
