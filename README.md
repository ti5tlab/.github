## About Us

**TI5Robot Technology Innovation Lab** is dedicated to exploring and advancing robotics technologies through engineering innovation, software development, and open-source collaboration.

Building on the robotics technology ecosystem of TI5Robot, we focus on the integration of robotic hardware, intelligent control systems, motion algorithms, and software platforms.

Our mission is to make robotics development more accessible, efficient, and collaborative by sharing practical tools, technical resources, and open-source projects with developers and researchers worldwide.

We believe that **Open Source Drives Innovation, and Innovation Shapes the Future of Robotics.**

## Research & Development

Our work spans several key areas of robotics technology:

| Research Area | Description |
|---|---|
| 🤖 Humanoid Robotics | Humanoid robot systems, motion control, and intelligent applications |
| ⚙️ Robotic Actuators | Joint modules, motor drivers, and actuator control systems |
| 🦾 Robotic Manipulation | Robotic arms, dexterous hands, and force-controlled manipulation |
| 🎮 Motion Control | Motor control, trajectory planning, and real-time control |
| 🧠 Embodied Intelligence | Robot perception, intelligent decision-making, and embodied AI |
| 🔗 Teleoperation | Human-robot interaction, motion retargeting, and remote operation |
| 🛠️ Robotics Software | SDKs, development tools, middleware, and system integration |

## Open Source Projects

We are committed to building an open and collaborative robotics ecosystem.

Our repositories cover:

- **Hardware & Drivers** — Hardware interfaces, motor drivers, and communication protocols.
- **Motion Control** — Motion planning, trajectory generation, and control algorithms.
- **Robot SDKs** — Development interfaces and software tools for robotic systems.
- **ROS & ROS 2** — Robotics middleware, packages, and integration examples.
- **Teleoperation** — Remote control, motion capture, and human-robot interaction.
- **AI & Robotics** — Exploring the integration of artificial intelligence and robotics.

Explore our repositories to discover tools, examples, and ongoing projects.

## Community & Collaboration

We welcome developers, researchers, engineers, and robotics enthusiasts to explore, contribute, and build together.

Whether you are developing robot control systems, integrating hardware, researching intelligent algorithms, or building robotics applications, we encourage you to participate in our open-source community.

**How to get involved:**

1. Explore our repositories and documentation.
2. Report bugs and share ideas through GitHub Issues.
3. Contribute improvements through Pull Requests.
4. Share your applications and technical experiences.

Every contribution helps move robotics forward.
